How it works

Loop. A procedure pack (data) creates the checklist: 12 shared items plus 2 per procedure, each with an owner and due time. A task is closed only with evidence or a documented clinician decision. Anything late is re-pinged to its owner, nudged to the patient, and escalated after a cap. Nothing is dropped silently.

Safety. Red flags are caught by fixed rules before anything else, in English, Bahasa Malaysia and Chinese. Clinical questions (blood thinners, antibiotics, flying) are never answered; they go to a named clinician. Unknown questions also go to a clinician, never a guess.

Model. Version 1 uses no AI model. A model could later help with free-text understanding, but only to escalate, never to downgrade or answer clinical questions.

Procedures.

UAE fit. The shared checklist includes insurance pre-authorisation. Integration with Malaffi/Sahatna would be an adapter on the result and appointment feeds; it is not built here.

Author's estimate, to be confirmed: how often a step is missed today is not yet measured. This prototype demonstrates behaviour, not clinical outcomes.
