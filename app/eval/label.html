Blind labelling of test messages

Fictional messages written by an AI. You see one at a time, with no label and no rule result. Your labels stay in this browser until you press Export.

How to label. Imagine this message arrives from a patient in the weeks around an elective knee or other orthopaedic operation. Choose what you would want to happen:

  • Emergency: call the ambulance or go to the Emergency Department now.
  • Same-day: a clinician should speak to or see the patient today.
  • Routine: can wait for normal clinic hours, or needs no clinical action.
  • Unsure: you cannot tell from the text, or you cannot read the language.

Label from the words alone. Do not open the Console or the rules while labelling. If two people label, do it separately and do not discuss until both have exported.

Opened as a file, not from the server: choose the pool file (make it with node app/eval/pool.js > pool.json).

Finished. Press Export and send the file to whoever runs node app/eval/score_labels.js yourfile.json.
