  4. Gaps to clarify
  5. To-do list with owner and due date

How it works

Loop. A procedure pack (data) creates the checklist: 12 shared items plus 2 per procedure, each with an owner and due time. A task is closed only with evidence or a documented clinician decision. Anything late is re-pinged to its owner, nudged to the patient, and escalated after a cap. Nothing is dropped silently.

Safety. Red flags are caught by fixed rules before anything else. Coverage is partial: at first pass most misses were in Chinese, and some English chest-pain wording and a Malay collapse message were missed too. Round-6 wording in Malay and Chinese is a machine draft pending native clinician review. Clinical questions (blood thinners, antibiotics, flying) are never answered; they go to a named clinician. Unknown questions also go to a clinician, never a guess.

Model. Scripted mode, the default, uses no AI model. An optional AI mode sends typed answers to a model you configure; its replies are checked before anyone sees them, and clinical questions are never answered by it.

Procedures.

UAE fit. The shared checklist includes insurance pre-authorisation. Integration with Malaffi/Sahatna would be an adapter on the result and appointment feeds; it is not built here.
