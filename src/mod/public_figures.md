## Summary for the prototype

1. These figures can justify treating no-shows (roughly 6% to 28% in the cited settings) and day-of-surgery cancellation (9.7% in one Indian multicentre study) as real problems worth designing for.
2. They cannot justify any Gulf/UAE or orthopaedic-specific rate, the share of cancellations caused by incomplete preparation, or any discharge or transition missed-result rate; those gaps remain open.
3. Use them only as placeholder sizing, and replace them with local clinic data before making any claim.
